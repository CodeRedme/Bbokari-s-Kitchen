Brownie Science

Each bar shows how much of the batter (by weight) is butter, sugar, flour, chocolate, cocoa and eggs.

What the numbers say

Vlog version: low flour, and lots of butter, sugar and chocolate. That points to fudgy. The bread flour may add a chewy edge, and only a little baking powder means it stays dense, not cakey.

Big-batch version: about half the flour share, and nearly four times as much sugar as flour. Expect it to be gooier and sweeter, close to molten.

How I counted: eggs at 50 g each, everything else by weight, small items like vanilla and salt left out. These are readings of the ratios, and the test bakes will show if they hold up.

About and sources

Fan project note

Bbokari's Kitchen is an unofficial fan project made with love. It isn't affiliated with Stray Kids, JYP Entertainment or Felix. Bbokari is a SKZOO character, and the character belongs to its original creators. I made the logo and wallpaper layouts myself in Canva.

Where the recipes come from

  • Fan-written transcriptions of the brownie recipe from Felix's kitchen vlog, shared on Tumblr and TikTok.
  • A baking blog that recreated the recipe and notes Felix didn't give exact quantities.
  • A TikTok caption saying Felix shared the big-batch version with a YouTuber. I couldn't verify that claim.

The versions disagree on some amounts, such as butter and chocolate, so I used the most common ones. Every ingredient amount here is an estimate until I test it.
